# Sand Casting

## What is Sand Casting?
Sand casting is a **metal casting manufacturing process** where **molten metal is poured into a mould made from sand**. Once the metal has cooled and solidified, the sand mould is broken away to reveal the finished casting.

It is one of the **oldest and most widely used casting processes**, suitable for **small batch and mass production** of metal components.

---

## Why Sand Casting is Used
Sand casting is used because it:
- Can produce **complex shapes**
- Is suitable for **large and small components**
- Has **low tooling costs**
- Can cast a wide range of metals
- Is flexible for design changes

It is commonly used in **engineering, automotive, and industrial products**.

---

## How Sand Casting Works
Sand casting uses a **single‑use sand mould**.

### Step‑by‑Step Process
1. A **pattern** (replica of the final shape) is made, usually from wood, plastic, or metal
2. The pattern is placed into a **mould box**
3. Special **casting sand** is packed tightly around the pattern
4. The pattern is removed, leaving a cavity
5. A **gating system** (channels) is formed
6. The mould is closed
7. **Molten metal** is poured into the mould
8. The metal cools and solidifies
9. The sand mould is broken away
10. The casting is cleaned and finished

---

## The Sand Mould
The mould is made from:
- **Silica sand**
- Clay or resin binders
- Water (in green sand moulds)

### Parts of a Sand Mould
- **Cope** – top half
- **Drag** – bottom half
- **Core** – creates hollow sections
- **Gates and runners** – guide molten metal
- **Riser** – allows gases to escape and metal to feed the casting

Each mould is **destroyed after use**, but the sand can often be recycled.

---

## Metals Suitable for Sand Casting
Sand casting can be used with many metals, including:

- **Cast iron**
- **Steel**
- **Aluminium**
- **Brass**
- **Bronze**

This versatility makes sand casting very popular.

## Key Properties of Sand Casting
| Feature | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Tooling Cost | Low |
| Production Volume | Low to high |
| Shape Complexity | High |
| Surface Finish | Rough |
| Dimensional Accuracy | Medium |
| Size Range | Very wide |

---

## Advantages of Sand Casting
- Low mould and tooling cost
- Suitable for large components
- Can produce complex internal shapes
- Wide range of metals can be used
- Flexible for design changes
- Recycled sand reduces waste

---

## Disadvantages of Sand Casting
- Rough surface finish
- Lower accuracy than die casting
- Labour intensive
- Requires machining after casting
- Slower than automated casting processes
- Each mould is single‑use

---

## Typical Uses in Product Design
Sand casting is commonly used for:

- **Engine blocks**
- **Machine bases**
- **Pipes and fittings**
- **Metal housings**
- **Brackets**
- **Automotive components**
- **Industrial parts**

It is ideal for **large or heavy components**.

---

## Sand Casting vs Die Casting
| Feature | Sand Casting | Die Casting |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Tooling Cost | Low | Very High |
| Surface Finish | Rough | Smooth |
| Accuracy | Medium | High |
| Production Speed | Slower | Very Fast |
| Metals Used | Many | Mainly aluminium & zinc |

---

## Sustainability and Environmental Impact
### Advantages
- Sand can be recycled
- Low energy tooling
- Suitable for recycled metals

### Disadvantages
- Energy‑intensive melting
- Dust and waste sand
- Emissions from molten metal

### Improvements
- Sand recycling systems
- Improved ventilation and filtration
- Energy‑efficient furnaces

---

## Health and Safety Considerations
- High temperatures from molten metal
- Risk of burns
- Dust from sand handling
- PPE required:
  - Heat‑resistant gloves
  - Face shields
  - Protective clothing
- Industrial process only
- Not suitable for school workshops

---

## Suitability for Product Design
Sand casting is suitable when:
- Large or complex metal parts are required
- Low tooling cost is important
- Small to medium batch production
- Strength and durability are required

Sand casting is not suitable when:
- Very high surface finish is required
- Tight tolerances are essential
- Thin sections are needed
- Fast mass production is required

## Overall Summary
Sand casting is a **versatile metal casting process** that uses **single‑use sand moulds** to form complex metal components. It is widely used because of its **low tooling cost, flexibility, and ability to cast large parts** from a wide range of metals. However, sand casting produces a **rough surface finish and lower dimensional accuracy** compared to other casting methods, often requiring additional machining. In A Level Product Design, sand casting should be evaluated in terms of its **manufacturing process, cost, material suitability, sustainability, and appropriateness for low to medium volume production of complex metal components**.
