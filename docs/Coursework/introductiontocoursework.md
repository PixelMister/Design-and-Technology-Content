### **INTRODUCTION: YOUR NEA PROJECT**

Your **Non-Examined Assessment (NEA)** is one of the most important parts of your A-Level Design and Technology qualification. 

It provides an opportunity to demonstrate your ability to work as a designer by identifying a genuine problem, investigating the needs of a client, developing an appropriate design solution, manufacturing a prototype and evaluating the success of your work.

Unlike a written examination, the NEA allows you to demonstrate your understanding through an extended, practical project. 

You will need to combine the knowledge you have gained from studying Design and Technology with your own creativity, technical understanding, manufacturing skills and ability to make informed decisions.


Your NEA contributes **50% of your final A-Level grade and is assessed out of 120 marks.**

The project is divided into four main parts, containing eleven assessment areas.
